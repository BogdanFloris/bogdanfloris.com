## 3-11-2026

Just upgraded this website to add this scribble system. It allows me to write my thoughts down without committing me to writing something long form. It's a system that attempts to get me over my writers block on longer essays. I never issues writing my thoughts down in short form as evidenced by my physical notebook, however I do struggle on essays. To remember to update this, I will try to go over what I wrote in a day in my physical notebook, see if something is worth pulling from it, and write it down here. If I notice that I have mused about the same thing over multiple days, I will have notes that I can then use to turn them into an essay.
